## Part 2: Turn on the daily article generator (browser, once per repo)

You need an Anthropic API key. If you don't have one:
1. Go to https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys
2. Click "Create Key", name it `article-generator`, copy the key (starts with `sk-ant-`).

Then for the **tinta** repo:
1. Go to https://github.com/charlieSAX/tinta/settings/secrets/actions
2. Click the green "New repository secret" button.
3. Name: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (exactly that). Secret: paste your key. Click "Add secret".
4. Go to https://github.com/charlieSAX/tinta/actions — if you see a banner saying workflows are disabled, click "I understand my workflows, go ahead and enable them".
5. Test it now: click "Daily articles" in the left sidebar, then the "Run workflow" dropdown on the right, then the green "Run workflow" button. Wait ~2 minutes, refresh; a green tick means new Spanish packs are in the feed.

Repeat the same five steps for **Naomilingo**:
- Secrets page: https://github.com/charlieSAX/Naomilingo/settings/secrets/actions (same key)
- Actions page: https://github.com/charlieSAX/Naomilingo/actions

From then on it runs automatically every morning at 05:30 UTC, even with your Mac off. Each run builds up to 4 new packs, grades its own output against a quality rubric, regenerates anything that fails, and publishes only the survivors. Cost is a few pence per day.

## Part 3: On the phones (you and Naomi)

1. Open the app. New articles arrive automatically on open (or tap "Get new articles").
2. The 15 starter articles already on the phone keep their old short summaries (the app never overwrites an article you already have). To get the new long versions: in the Library, tap the bin icon on an article, then tap "Get new articles"; it returns with the 150-word summary and audio.
3. For the best voice (one-time, on each phone):
   - iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Spanish (or Italian) → download an **Enhanced** voice (e.g. Mónica Enhanced / Alice Enhanced).
   - Android: Settings → search "Text-to-speech" → Speech Recognition and Synthesis → install Spanish/Italian voice data.
4. In the app: Settings → Audio → pick the enhanced voice → Test.
