## Part 2: First run of the daily article task (in Cowork, 2 minutes)

The daily generator is a Cowork scheduled task called **daily-language-articles**. No API key, no cloud: Claude writes the packs here on your subscription and publishes from this Mac. It runs every day at 12:05 while Cowork is open (if Cowork was closed, it catches up next time you open it).

Do this once, after Part 1:
1. In Cowork, open the **Scheduled** section in the sidebar.
2. Click **daily-language-articles**, then **Run now**.
3. Approve any permission prompts it shows (it needs to run the deploy command on your Mac). Approvals are remembered, so future runs never ask again.
4. The run summary lists the new article titles; the apps pick them up on next open.

Each run: 3 new Spanish + 3 new Italian packs, level-calibrated, self-checked (word counts, schema, quality cold read) before anything is published.

## Part 3: On the phones (you and Naomi)

1. Open the app. New articles arrive automatically on open (or tap "Get new articles").
2. The 15 starter articles already on the phone keep their old short summaries (the app never overwrites an article you already have). To get the new long versions: in the Library, tap the bin icon on an article, then tap "Get new articles"; it returns with the 150-word summary and audio.
3. For the best voice (one-time, on each phone):
   - iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Spanish (or Italian) → download an **Enhanced** voice (e.g. Mónica Enhanced / Alice Enhanced).
   - Android: Settings → search "Text-to-speech" → Speech Recognition and Synthesis → install Spanish/Italian voice data.
4. In the app: Settings → Audio → pick the enhanced voice → Test.
